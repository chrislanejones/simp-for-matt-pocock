Transferable — the whole list

ArrayBuffer · AudioData · ImageBitmap · MediaSourceHandle · MediaStreamTrack · MessagePort · MIDIAccess · OffscreenCanvas · ReadableStream · RTCDataChannel · TransformStream · VideoFrame · WebTransportReceiveStream · WebTransportSendStream · WritableStream

Not on that list

Typed arrays (Uint8Array, Uint8ClampedArray, …) are serializable but not transferable. Transfer x.buffer, never x. And the transferred thing must also appear in the message body — the transfer list says how to send, not what.
